## Data availability

In this file, I document the availability of the datasets for the re-analysis of the study from Munoz *et al.* (Science, 2026)

### Dysbiosis cohorts

- **IBD**: https://ibdmdb.org/results (I believe it is HMP2, but it is not mentioned in the supp. material). Original paper in https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-018-0176-y.
- **IBS**: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/29n2z5r5ph/3. Original paper in: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32916129/
- **CRC**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-019-0458-7 
- **CDI**: https://github.com/grp-bork/cdifficile_Ferretti_2022/tree/main/data/CDI 

### Healthy cohorts
